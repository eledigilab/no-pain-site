023|The idea
024|From signal4help to <span class="hl">digital4help™</span>
025|The hand signal, signal4help, has become the universal gesture for asking for help in silence when in danger. But it only works if someone sees it. no pAIn™ has created an electronic version: the “signal for help” reaches the right people, wherever they are, together with your location.

034|A keyword spoken out loud, a tap on the screen, on the watch or on a button in your pocket. The app does the rest and alerts your emergency contacts.
035|Why it matters
036|In danger, you cannot always speak.
037|112 and apps such as YouPol remain essential tools. But they require you to talk to an operator, explain what is happening and say where you are. With an attacker next to you, that is often impossible.

413|no pAIn™ — Technology and research
414|The technology and scientific publications of the no pAIn™ project by Prof. Eng. Agostino Giorgio, Politecnico di Bari.
415|From research <span class="hl">to the request for help.</span>
416|no pAIn™ means <em>never oppressed protected by Artificial Intelligence Non violence system</em>. The project by Prof. Eng. Agostino Giorgio, lecturer at the Politecnico di Bari, applies artificial intelligence, speech recognition and geolocation to requests for help sent to trusted contacts.
417|Architecture
418|From your device to your contacts
419|The project describes an architecture with no account, login or proprietary cloud. The developer states that no personal data is collected; settings and contacts stay on the device. External services provided by the app are still used to deliver alerts and determine the address.
420|On-device speech recognition
421|In the Android versions and in the enabled languages, keyword recognition also works offline. Sending alerts, however, requires the connections needed by the chosen channel: mobile data, SMS or telephony.
422|Reliability
423|Continuity and recovery
424|The presentation describes resending alerts when the network returns, resuming live location after interruptions and guided reactivation after restarts or updates, with tests on real smartphones and smartwatches. Actual operation depends on version, permissions, network and device.

460|Why not call 112 directly?
461|112 remains essential and, if you can, call it. But in danger it is often impossible to talk to an operator, explain the situation and say where you are. That is exactly what no pAIn™ apps are for: with a word or a discreet tap they alert two trusted people with your location, and they will call the police.
462|Who receives the help request?
